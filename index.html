FIELD NOTES

A little context before you register.

What “unconfirmed” means

The listing does not establish whether results go to DUPR, or its statements conflict. It doesn’t mean results won’t be reported. “Not DUPR-recorded” means the organizer explicitly says results will not be uploaded. Rating-based eligibility and result reporting are separate things.

Distance & drive time

Mileage and drive times are rough planning estimates from central Cedar Park, not live navigation. The 40-mile view is distance-based; the two-hour view is based on a normal-traffic estimate. Check live directions before leaving.

What counts as 3.0

Some organizers name a 3.0 bracket. Others use combined-team caps, such as under 6 or combined 7.0. The filters include both when a 3.0 player can reasonably fit, and the card spells out the actual rule.
