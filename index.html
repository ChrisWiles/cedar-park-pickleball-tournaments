FIELD NOTES

A little context before you register.

What “unconfirmed” means

The listing does not establish whether results go to DUPR, or its statements conflict. It doesn’t mean results won’t be reported. “Not DUPR-recorded” means the organizer explicitly says results will not be uploaded.

Distance and drive time

Mileage and drive times are rough planning estimates from central Cedar Park, not live navigation. Check live directions before leaving.

How bracket matching works

Some organizers use exact skill brackets. Others publish combined-team caps or broader bands. “Compatible combined bracket” means the published structure can fit that level, not that an exact bracket is guaranteed.
